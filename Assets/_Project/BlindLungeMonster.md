# Blind Lunge Monster

Installed in Nights 3, 4 and 5. Reusable prefab: `Assets/Prefabs/BlindLungeMonster.prefab`. The Visual child now contains the downloaded textured Crawl model, replacing the labeled cube. The root NetworkObject, CharacterController, hearing, speed, attacks and difficulty tuning are preserved.

The host controls hearing, movement, deaths, searches, inventory and rock impacts. The monster has no sight or silent-player tracking. It roams aimlessly until it hears a sound. Footsteps, sprinting, landings, in-game speech and rock impacts are audible within 40 m. A perfectly still, silent player does not update its target; it wanders around the last heard position. Solid walls muffle sound. The PA anomaly also attracts it. In-game voice speech detection sends only the speaking player's location, with rate limiting; external voice apps cannot be detected.

It commits to a straight lunge of up to 10 m at 32 m/s, after a 0.35 s windup, then recovers for 1.25 s. Walls stop the lunge. Contact during a lunge kills through the existing death/spectator flow. It remembers a sound for eight seconds and stops when it reaches the sound or the memory expires. Every lunge plays a spatial sound. A rock impact immediately redirects it and takes priority over all player sounds for five seconds. A faint 3D heartbeat fades out by 26 m.

Look at nearby ground and hold E for one second to search. Each completed search has a 10% success chance and can start only once every 2.5 seconds. The result says Found one small rock or Nothing found and fades. Releasing E, looking away from reachable ground, opening a menu, or dying interrupts the search without a roll. Release E before starting another attempt. The host validates held-input heartbeats throughout the search. There are no placed collectible rocks.

Carry one small rock at a time. A found rock is physically attached to the first-person camera at the bottom left, like the bunker tablet. The textured weathered stone has subtle movement bob and a softly lit first-person render pass, keeping it readable in darkness and clear of wall clipping. The held and thrown rock use the same 16 cm model. Left-click throws it along the view direction and consumes it. The host simulates the projectile and detects its first impact; all peers receive the same launch and landing, and only that authoritative impact creates distraction noise for Blind Lunge.

Controls are suppressed while menus are open, dead, or not the local owner. Requests cannot choose another player's inventory or search/throw origin. Searches are limited to ground near the actual feet, independently of where the player model pivot is placed.

Tools > Blind Lunge > Check Core Behaviour runs isolated core checks. Logs/BlindLungeChecksResult.txt contains results. The request-driven BlindLungePlayChecks runs an offline play-mode integration test without saving test changes into production scenes. A live host/client session remains necessary to verify multiplayer input/latency, contact deaths and audio balance together.

## Crawl model and animation

The original `Running Crawl (1).fbx` and Ch25 texture maps are organized under `Assets/Art/Monsters/Crawl`, retaining their existing GUIDs. Both embedded takes are configured to loop. The monster uses the 0.667 second `mixamo.com` running-crawl cycle, extracted as the in-place `CrawlLoop.anim`; the extra `Take 001` remains available in the source model. Two Standard skin materials bind the matching diffuse and correctly imported normal maps.

`BlindCrawlAnimator` drives the visual Animator motion-time parameter from the synchronized NGO server clock. Host, clients and late joiners sample the current loop rather than depending on local spawn time. It adds no NetworkBehaviour or animation RPCs. Root motion and imported model colliders are disabled so animation never competes with authoritative monster movement. The visual is grounded using the lowest sampled skinned-mesh point across the cycle. Tools > Blind Lunge > Install Crawl Model regenerates the visual assets and updates the reusable prefab and all three night scenes.

Crawl verification: production and multiplayer fixture builds succeeded with zero errors. Four production/prefab visuals passed bone, material, loop, collision and scene-ID validation. The graphics pose sheet is `Logs/BlindCrawl-sheet.png`. The host/client/late-join run passed 44 assertions, including moving skeletons, repeated loops, synchronized phase, preserved hearing/distraction and owner-specific contact deaths; all three logs contained no managed, spawn, synchronization or animation errors.
